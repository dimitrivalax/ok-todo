Privacy Policy — Ok! Todo

Last updated: June 25, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the mobile application Ok! Todo (package id fr.ok.todo, the "App") handles your information. We care deeply about your privacy. In short: the App does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

1. No personal data collection

The App is a simple to-do list. It requires no account, no sign-up and no login. We do not collect, store on any server, or transmit any personal data about you.

2. Data stored locally on your device

All information you enter (task titles, dates, times, and preferences such as reminder time) is saved only locally on your device, in the App's private storage.

  • This data never leaves your device.
  • It is accessible to no one but you.
  • It is removed when you delete your tasks or uninstall the App.

3. Local notifications

The App may send you local notifications to remind you of your tasks. These notifications are generated and scheduled entirely on your device. No remote (push) notification service is used, and no information is sent off your device for this purpose.

You can allow or deny notifications at any time in your device settings.

4. No sharing with third parties

The App contains no advertising, no analytics, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs used for tracking. No data is shared, sold or rented to third parties.

5. Permissions requested

  • Notifications: to display local reminders about your tasks.

The App requests no other sensitive permissions (no location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or call logs).

6. Children

The App is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect any data from them. Since no personal data is collected, the App is safe for everyone to use.

7. Security

Because your data stays on your device, its security mainly depends on the protection of your device (passcode, biometrics, etc.). We recommend securing access to your device.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

9. Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us at: dimitri@valax.fr.
